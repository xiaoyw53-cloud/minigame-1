# Minigame 1
## Devlog
1.To me, Unity is like putting together a smartphone. A Component is a single app, like the camera or speaker. A Game Object is just an empty phone—it needs those apps to actually do anything.
2.I think that The Scene is the home screen that brings all your apps and icons together in one place. It’s what makes the phone come alive and easy to use. It’s the full environment that brings the whole phone to life.
